Both new PNG previews were visually inspected after the final short category labels were applied. The root agent separately inspected both. The figures use the existing orx style adapter and actual LaTeX Computer Modern / CM-Super fonts at a 4.8-inch printed width. Plot PDFs and SVGs are vector assets; PNGs are previews.

- Main comparison: the three primary mechanisms and the shared-weight candidate control remain visible. Logarithmic time resolves early changes; the distance axis includes zero. The plain and gated Gaussian curves are close because their measured difference is modest. They have not been artificially separated. Probability curves and their bootstrap envelopes remain bounded. A small display margin below zero prevents positive near-zero MSE markers from being clipped; negative MSE is not plotted.
- Supplement: reference compression, effective cross-group weights, mixture-versus-MAP readout, and the three distinct distance summaries have clear panel labels. The actual gate-weight range determines its vertical extent; weights above the nominal ungated total would not be clipped. The descriptive ensemble-mean norm has no confidence whisker. Short category words avoid the overlap detected and corrected by the initial strict layout audit.
- All curves retain all 5,000 updates. No smoothing, outcome-based seed selection, or display-only data transformation changes the measured trajectories. Fifty paired seeds, rather than agents or time points, are the uncertainty units.
- Standalone captions define all overloaded line styles: information-pool solid / moment-match dashed in supplement (a), gas solid / mass dotted in (b), mixture solid / MAP dashed in (c). They identify the fixed settings, candidate archive reuse, late reporting window, interval construction, and descriptive-only orange triangle.
- The initial supplemental caption exceeded its booklet page by two lines. It was shortened without removing these definitions. The final captioned booklet has nine pages: seven unchanged prior figure PDFs followed by the new comparison and supplement. The final automated check also verifies physical width, real CM fonts, vector content, bounds, hashes and page count.
