\large Paradigm Dynamics as Structure Learning over a Hidden World}
\author{Jonas Hallgren \and David Hyland \and Mahault Albarracin%
\thanks{Author order and affiliations provisional. Draft for IWAI 2026.}}
\date{Draft --- \today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A scientist is not handed the variables of the world, nor the dependencies among them: the structure
of reality is hidden, and a community must \emph{learn} that structure even as it uses it. We model a
paradigm as a directed network of commitments and a scientific community as a population of bounded
active-inference agents performing \emph{Bayesian structure learning} on that network---expanding it
by proposing new commitments and reducing it by pruning the dependencies the data do not support. One
operator organizes the formalism: the propagation operator of the network carries a
\emph{conservatism} field (the cost of revising a commitment) and a \emph{conviction} field (the
value a bounded agent attaches to holding it), the same operator applied to two different sources.
Running the model through the full Kuhnian cycle---normal science, anomaly, crisis, reduction,
discovery---in a 1{,}816-run sweep yields three findings. The lock-in boundary sits at \emph{very}
weak social coupling: conviction-driven gating of disconfirming evidence cannot defeat connection,
and only isolation protects a superseded paradigm. Discovery of a genuinely unconceived dimension
follows a Poisson waiting-time law, but a \emph{staggered} discovery does not survive the communal
prior---survival is zero at slow proposal rates and appears only once arrivals cluster the
community's wakings within a few steps of one another, a critical mass in \emph{time}. And the predicted belt-first/core-last staircase is a creature of \emph{observability},
not of a revision-rate gradient: it appears when the core commitments carry no direct observation
rows---evidence reaching them only through relational channels---and vanishes under full
observability, which localizes what a richer model must add to data-dependent couplings.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: changing a frame, not a credence}
\label{sec:background}
% ======================================================================

To change one's mind about a fact is to update a belief; to change a paradigm is to change the
\emph{frame} in which beliefs are posed. Such a frame is not a credence but a \emph{structure}: a
network of conditional dependencies through which information propagates. A community that only
revised its credences, leaving the network fixed, could not change frame at all. This is the
phenomenon Kuhn named but could not run forward \citep{kuhn1962}: nothing in \emph{The Structure of
Scientific Revolutions} tells us, mechanistically, why a community holds a structure of commitments
long past the first disconfirming evidence, nor what finally makes the holding give way.

When the chemical community released phlogiston it did not revise a single credence. It rewrote a
network of commitments in which combustion, calcination, respiration, and the theory of acids were
all load-bearing on a hub that had to go: late-phlogistic theorists proposed cascading revisions that
proved mutually incompatible \citep{blumenthal2017,holmes2000}, defending less a hypothesis about
combustion than a constellation of entangled commitments \citep{boantza2011}---phlogiston was
load-bearing for too much.

The dominant formalism for such communities, network epistemology, cannot represent this case. Its
agents hold a scalar credence in a fixed proposition and update on a trust graph
\citep{zollman2010,oconnor2019,zollman2013}, a programme that has produced striking results on
transient diversity, polarization, and the division of cognitive labour
\citep{jonard2025,michelini2023,kitcher1990,strevens2003}---but the phlogiston puzzle is not a failure
to converge on a number. The commitments were \emph{entangled}: revising any one forced a re-reading
of the others, and the disagreement was about which entanglements to keep. Thagard's ECHO
\citep{thagard1989,thagard1990} captures the structural intuition as undirected coherence; it lacks
the directed dependencies, the carry-over cost of mind-change, and the expand/reduce asymmetry we
derive below. Active-inference accounts of epistemic communities supply an endogenous precision-based
lock-in mechanism \citep{albarracin2022} and warn that naive aggregation degrades collective precision
\citep{friston2023federated,waade2025}; the variational cost of mind-change has been formalized for a
single agent as a motivated trade between revision cost and the utility of a held belief
\citep{hyland2025}. What none of these does is treat \emph{belief structure itself} as the object of
inference: they retain a fixed menu of hypotheses, and a scalar credence over a menu cannot say how
revising one commitment forces a re-reading of the rest, nor how a community arrives at a \emph{new}
way of carving up the world. When only a number moves on the trust graph, a community can converge
on it, herd on it, or split over it---but its members can never come to hold different \emph{kinds}
of world, only different values on a shared one. The question, made concrete in \S\ref{sec:model},
is therefore what moves: this paper lets a piece of \emph{structure} travel, so that one agent can
come to represent a commitment its peers do not.

We make structure the object. Agents carry a directed network of commitments, learn its structure by
Bayesian model expansion and reduction, and are \emph{bounded}: they sample a complex world through
narrow channels, value some conclusions over others, and judge what they receive against their own
model. Section~\ref{sec:bmebmr} builds the model and states the simulation loop explicitly;
Section~\ref{sec:results} reports a single-agent dark-energy net and a 1{,}816-run population sweep
through the full Kuhnian cycle on the phlogiston world.

\section{Why paradigms are networks under structure learning}
\label{sec:hidden}
% ======================================================================

\paragraph{The structure of reality is hidden.}
A scientist confronting a new domain is not given the right variables, and is certainly not given the
dependencies among them: she does not know whether two regularities share a hidden common cause, are
lawfully coupled, or merely coincide. For an active-inference reader the move is natural: just as
agents infer hidden states, they must infer the hidden \emph{dependencies among} those states. The
cognitive-science literature on theory acquisition reaches the same conclusion---conceptual
development as hierarchical Bayesian program induction, a stochastic search over structures
\citep{ullman2020,kemp2008form,ullman2012}.

\paragraph{To explore is to expand; to do normal science is to reduce.}
Network-epistemology models keep their communities from collapsing onto the first attractive theory
with a hand-tuned exploration term---an $\epsilon$, a temperature, a switching cost
\citep{zollman2010,oconnor2019}. Once structure is the object of inference, the \emph{content} of
exploration is no longer a tuned bonus but the epistemic value of expanding the model to entertain a
commitment not yet in it \citep{friston2015,friston2017curiosity}; what stays free is only the
\emph{rate} at which proposals arrive. The complementary operation is Bayesian model reduction:
pruning structure the data do not support, in closed form, at the slowest timescale of the learning
hierarchy \citep{friston2018bmr,smith2022plos}. Existing applications of this machinery run on single
agents \citep{smith2022plos,priorelli2023} or define ``group'' as between-subject neuroimaging
\citep{friston2016peb}; treating a paradigm as the object a \emph{community} jointly reduces and
expands is the open move this paper makes.

\paragraph{Two fields, one operator.}
The single non-obvious step the rest of the paper turns on: once a paradigm is a network, one
operation that network science already owns (\S\ref{sec:carryover})---propagating influence from a
commitment to everything downstream---generates \emph{two} fields on the same graph, depending on
what is propagated. Propagate sheer dependence and we get
\emph{conservatism}: a commitment is costly to revise because much depends on it. Propagate
\emph{value} and we get \emph{conviction}: the worth a bounded agent attaches to holding a belief,
carried through the same dependencies. The two are independent---a commitment can be central yet
value-neutral, cheap yet cherished (Fig.~\ref{fig:twofields}). That attitudes live on a network is
the empirical posture of the Causal Attitude Network model \citep{dalege2016}; we place it on the
same \emph{directed} graph that carries the cost of revision.

\section{The model}
\label{sec:bmebmr}
% ======================================================================

We build the object up from the single agent and then state the population dynamics as an explicit
per-round loop (\S\ref{sec:model}).

\subsection{A paradigm network and its operator}
\label{sec:carryover}

An agent represents a paradigm as a directed network $G=(V,E,\Pi)$: nodes carry theoretical
commitments $x_v$, edges carry inferential dependencies, and each edge bears a coupling precision
$\pi_{uv}$ measuring how tightly $v$ is bound to $u$. This is just a generative model whose hidden
states are the commitments themselves, with precision playing its usual role: a high-$\pi$ edge says
that belief in $u$ strongly constrains belief in $v$. Internal coherence is the corresponding
precision-weighted graphical model, $p(x\mid G)\propto\prod_{(u\to v)\in
E}\psi_{uv}(x_u,x_v)^{\pi_{uv}}$. None of this machinery is ours: it is a linear-Gaussian structural
equation model in the lineage of Wright's path analysis \citep{wright1934}, edges as path
coefficients and precisions as their weights. We work
throughout in that \emph{linear-Gaussian} regime, which puts every quantity below in closed form
(Appendix~\ref{app:operator}); agents are \emph{honest}---no one misreports, so lock-in is never a
dishonesty artifact---and \emph{bounded}, minimizing variational free energy step by step
\citep{dacosta2020} rather than planning over policies.

Revising a commitment is not a local act: changing $x_v$ forces everything that depends on it to
re-equilibrate. Picture the cascade. A nudge at one node travels along each outgoing edge, weighted
by that edge's precision; from each child it travels on to the grandchildren, picking up their
couplings; and so on down every chain of dependence. If $A$ is the matrix of edge precisions, then
$A^k$ collects the chains of length $k$, and summing chains of every length gives
$\sum_k A^k=(I-A)^{-1}$ (a finite sum, since a DAG has no cycles to traverse forever). We did not
have to invent this object; two literatures already own it. Path analysis knows it as the
\emph{total-effects matrix}: what a unit change at one node does to every descendant once all the
indirect routes are counted \citep{wright1934,bollen1987}. Network science knows the same sum as
the Katz--Bonacich index, where it measures how much of the graph a node reaches through walks of
all lengths \citep{katz1953,bonacich1987}; and it is exactly how influence travels between beliefs
in precision-weighted Gaussian models \citep{malioutov2006}. We adopt it as the \emph{propagation
operator} $\Top=(I-A)^{-1}$, indexed with rows as sources and columns as descendants, so the entry
$\Top_{iw}=\pi_{i\rw w}$ is the total precision-weighted coupling propagated from $i$ to $w$ along
all directed paths (Appendix~\ref{app:operator}).

Conservatism is then recognized rather than posited. Place a unit of weight on every node and ask,
for each commitment $i$, how much total weight a revision of $i$ would disturb. That is the operator
applied to the unit source,
\begin{equation}
\kappa \;=\; \Top\,\bone,
\qquad
\kappa_i \;=\;1+\!\!\sum_{w\in\desc(i)}\!\!\pi_{i\rw w},
\label{eq:kappa}
\end{equation}
the precision-weighted mass of a node's descendants. In network-science terms this is a centrality
score---a node scores high when many strong chains of dependence leave it (formally, the broadcast
variant of the Katz index)---and centrality is usually read as influence or status. We read the same
number as \emph{revision cost}: a commitment is exactly as expensive to revise as the mass that must
re-equilibrate when it moves. Conservatism is thus a centrality the graph already determines, not a
disposition assigned by hand.

What we add to this inherited machine comes in two layers. The first is contact with the world. The
dense, high-precision interior of the network is the \emph{core}; the sparse periphery in contact
with observation is the \emph{belt}, each belt node reading the world through a sensory channel of
precision $\rho_k$. The core carries no direct sensory channel: an interior commitment is revised
only by error propagated inward from the belt, through the same operator---costly by
Eq.~\eqref{eq:kappa} and insulated by position, which is what makes it slow.

The second layer is value, and the operator is run twice. Scientists are bounded agents who value
some conclusions over others---a result on which a career rests is held more tightly than its
evidence warrants \citep{kitcher1990,strevens2003}. Following the variational-cost account of belief
revision \citep{hyland2025}, we attach to each node an intrinsic utility $u_i$ and let it propagate
through the \emph{same} operator: a commitment one wants true lends value to its dependents, so the
conviction field is
\begin{equation}
U \;=\; \Top\,u,
\qquad
U_i \;=\; u_i+\!\!\sum_{w\in\desc(i)}\!\!\pi_{i\rw w}\,u_w .
\label{eq:U}
\end{equation}
In the operator's path-analytic reading, $U$ is the total effect of wanting things true. Where the
inherited models propagate a single source, a paradigm carries two: $\Top\bone$ and $\Top u$ are
linearly independent unless $u\propto\bone$, so the two fields need not align
(Fig.~\ref{fig:twofields}), and Appendix~\ref{app:darkenergy} exhibits the misalignment computed on
a real net.

\subsection{Motivated revision and the lock-in parameter}
\label{sec:revision}

The bounded agent revises its belief $q(x)$ over the whole network by trading fidelity to the honest
posterior against the value of where the commitments land,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{F}[q]\;=\;\KL{q(x)}{p(x\mid o,G)}\;-\;\lambda\,\Eq{q}{U^{\!\top}\!x},
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
q_\lambda(x)\;\propto\;p(x\mid o,G)\,e^{\lambda\,U^{\!\top}\!x},
\label{eq:tilted}
\end{equation}
with $\lambda$ a single temperature governing how far value may bend the evidence. The operation
itself is classical---an exponential tilt of the posterior, the form KL-regularized utility
maximization always yields \citep{todorov2009,ortega2013}; what is ours is the source of the tilt, a
conviction field propagated through the paradigm's own operator. In the linear-Gaussian regime the
tilt is a pure mean shift, displacing the commitments by $\lambda\Sigma U$ and leaving the
covariance untouched (Appendix~\ref{app:operator}). What opposes the displacement is
evidence, also through the mean: a disconfirming channel $k$ with sensory precision $\rho_k$ and
read-out $H_k$ pulls the mean back with gain $\propto\rho_k\,H_k\Sigma U$. As conviction drives a
disconfirming channel toward $\rho_k\to0$ that gain vanishes: the channel can no longer move the
mean, and the value-displaced commitment stands unrefuted. The fraction of disconfirming sensory
precision so silenced,
\begin{equation}
\gamma\;=\;\frac{\sum_{k\in\mathrm{dis}}\rho_k\,\mathbb{1}[\,\rho_k\ \text{gated by the core}\,]}
{\sum_{k\in\mathrm{dis}}\rho_k},
\label{eq:gamma}
\end{equation}
is the \emph{lock-in parameter}. In the experiments $\gamma$ is endogenous: each round the weight on a
disconfirming row is a saturating function of the conviction field projected onto it,
$w_k=\exp(-g\,|U\!\cdot\!H_k|)$, with $g$ the conviction-to-censorship coupling---no channel is gated
by hand.

\subsection{Structure learning: reduction and expansion}
\label{sec:structurelearning}

Structure learning revises $G$ itself through two asymmetric operations
\citep{friston2017curiosity,smith2020structure}, and the asymmetry is the formal counterpart of normal
versus revolutionary science.

\emph{Reduction} is the exact half. Each edge is parameterized by a prior precision $\alpha_e$
($\alpha_e\to\infty$ pins the edge's parameter to zero; $\alpha_e\to0$ frees it), the
automatic-relevance-determination parameterization of sparse Bayesian learning
\citep{mackay1992,tipping2001}. Bayesian model reduction \citep{friston2011,friston2018bmr} scores any
pruning in closed form from the already-inverted posterior,
\begin{equation}
\frac{p(o\mid\tilde M)}{p(o\mid M)}=\Eq{p(\theta\mid o)}{\frac{\tilde p(\theta)}{p(\theta)}},
\label{eq:bmr}
\end{equation}
the Savage--Dickey ratio in general form: one inversion scores every candidate reduced structure
without re-touching the data.

\emph{Expansion} reaches what reduction cannot: a commitment no one has represented. Following
\citet{friston2017curiosity}, the agent entertains a new node by wiring it tentatively to existing
nodes with free priors ($\alpha_e\to0$) and letting reduction prune the proposal back to the couplings
the data support (Fig.~\ref{fig:bmebmr}). Expansion is not closed-form---a new node may enlarge the
likelihood's support and requires a real inversion---and this cost asymmetry is why a field
reorganizes its periphery continually and its centre only rarely. The agent does not plan \emph{when}
to expand: candidates arrive at a Poisson rate, and an arrival is kept only if the model log Bayes
factor favours the wired-in node (Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery}). It must be a rate,
because one cannot plan toward a dimension one has not yet conceived---Stanford's problem of
unconceived alternatives \citep{stanford2006}, to which we return.

\subsection{Forgetting}
\label{sec:temporal}

Read once from fixed precisions, $\kappa=\Top\bone$ can only grow as learning adds edges: a paradigm
with no forgetting is a ratchet, and lock-in under it is automatic rather than earned. We import the
standard remedy unmodified: accumulated precision relaxes toward the prior between updates,
$\Pi\leftarrow\Pi_0+\omega(\Pi-\Pi_0)$ with forgetting factor $\omega\in(0,1]$
\citep{friston2017process,smith2022tutorial}, so a deposit $k$ steps old carries weight $\omega^k$.
The factor is the agent's prior on the world's structural drift---one forgets at the rate one believes
the structure is moving \citep{mathys2011}. The operator inherits the time index,
$\Top_t=(I-A_t)^{-1}$, and a once-central node whose couplings go unreinforced relaxes back toward
cheapness. With forgetting, the value tilt saturates at $\lambda U/(1-\omega)$ and competes with the
also-saturated per-step evidence, so conviction holds only above a threshold: an entrenched bloc
re-tracks a moving truth at low $\lambda$ and stays locked at high---motivated persistence turned
from a posited bias into a threshold conviction must clear.

\subsection{The population, and the simulation at a glance}
\label{sec:model}
% ======================================================================

A population of $N$ agents inhabits a shared hidden world, each carrying its own paradigm network
with its own coupling precisions, conviction field $U=\Top u$, and derived conservatism
$\kappa=\Top\bone$. Agents are coupled on a trust graph: communities are blocks of a stochastic block
model, with dense intra-community edges and an inter-community coupling weight (\emph{inter}) that we
sweep from total isolation to dense contact. Beliefs and accumulated precisions are fused across
trust edges in information form---a precision-weighted pooling of posteriors in which an agent's own
evidence and its neighbours' carry weight in proportion to trust, the information-form counterpart
of trust-weighted averaging in opinion dynamics \citep{degroot1974,acemoglu2011}---and what is not
standard is that structural edits spread the same way: an agent whose neighbour has awakened a new node receives the proposal and scores it
against its own model by Eq.~\eqref{eq:bmr}. Figure~\ref{fig:population} draws the picture: what
travels on the trust graph is not a number but a piece of structure, evaluated against wiring the
sender does not see.

Concretely, the experiments of \S\ref{sec:res-kuhn} instantiate the phlogiston world: a hub-structured
paradigm net in which a single core commitment (phlogiston) is load-bearing for combustion,
calcination and respiration; at a fixed step $t_{\mathrm{shift}}$ the world's regime flips (the calx
is heavier---the gravimetric anomaly), and the truth thereafter contains a dimension (oxygen) that is
\emph{not in any agent's network}. One round of the simulation, for every agent:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{Observe.} The world emits observations at the belt; channel weights on disconfirming
rows are set endogenously by the conviction gate, $w_k=\exp(-g\,|U\!\cdot\!H_k|)$
(\S\ref{sec:revision}), scaled by the agent's gate strength $s$.
\item \textbf{Infer.} The agent updates its (value-tilted, Eq.~\ref{eq:tilted}) posterior over $x$ by
exact linear-Gaussian inference and accumulates precision.
\item \textbf{Crisis check (reduction).} Each candidate prune is scored on the move ledger: the
closed-form prune evidence $\Delta F$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:bmr}) plus $\lambda_i$ times the equally
closed-form conviction-value change of the prune,
$\Delta U = U^{\!\top}\!(\mu_{\mathrm{reduced}}-\mu)$, read from the reduced posterior the
Savage--Dickey identity already exhibits. Both protective belt edges scoring
$\Delta F + \lambda_i\,\Delta U > 0$ is a \emph{crisis}, and the agent applies the reduction---the
hub is released (Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery}).
\item \textbf{Discovery check (expansion).} After reduction, the residual the old paradigm absorbed
becomes visible as a coherent pattern in prediction error; when a Poisson proposal arrives (rate
$r$), its \emph{direction} is read from that pattern's leading eigenvector, and the wake is
accepted iff the model log Bayes factor is positive---the data must hold the new node
(\S\ref{sec:structurelearning}, Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery}).
\item \textbf{Fuse.} Posteriors and structural edits are pooled across the trust graph, weighted by
the intra/inter coupling.
\item \textbf{Forget.} Accumulated precision decays by $\omega$ (\S\ref{sec:temporal}).
\end{enumerate}

Crisis, revolution and discovery are thus not stipulated events but threshold crossings of the
model's own evidence; Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery} derives both checks from the single
tilted objective and lists explicitly what remains a modelling commitment.
Table~\ref{tab:dials} lists the dials swept; everything else is held fixed, and
all code is seeded and ships its own null and positive controls.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
% ======================================================================

All results are from the population of \S\ref{sec:model} run through the full Kuhnian cycle on the
phlogiston world, swept overnight over the dials of Table~\ref{tab:dials} (1{,}816 healthy runs).
The single-agent \emph{dark-energy} net that realises the two fields of Fig.~\ref{fig:twofields} on
a worked example---and recovers the $\Omega_m$--$\Lambda$ degeneracy as a Schur complement, a
quantity a scalar credence has no room to express---is reported in Appendix~\ref{app:darkenergy}.
The nets are hand-wired to expose the geometry, not fitted to data: nothing here is evidence about
chemistry or cosmology, only about how a structured, bounded, valued mind changes. We close with
limitations and future work (\S\ref{sec:res-staircase}).

\subsection{The full cycle, endogenously: the phlogiston sweep}
\label{sec:res-kuhn}

In the population, the whole Kuhnian arc---normal science, the gravimetric anomaly, crisis,
reduction, discovery of oxygen, new normal---unfolds as successive threshold crossings of the agents'
own evidence (\S\ref{sec:model}), with no phase stipulated. The sweep then asks where each beat of the
arc lives in the dial space, and three findings organize it.

\paragraph{Even a trace of outside contact ends the old paradigm; only total isolation protects it.}
The first question the sweep answers: how much contact with the rest of the world can a dogmatic
community tolerate and still keep phlogiston? Almost none. The setup pits a dogmatic community---high
conviction, which makes its agents gate their own disconfirming channels (\S\ref{sec:revision})---
against an open vanguard already tracking the anomaly, and dials the trust weight between the two
communities (\emph{inter}) from $0$ (sealed off) up to dense contact. Sealed off, the protection
works: under strong gating ($s=10$) only $19\%$ of the dogmatic community ever converts to oxygen.
But the seal has to be perfect. At \emph{inter}$=0.005$---trust toward outsiders two hundred times
weaker than trust within the community---$92\%$ convert, no matter how strong the gate,
and making the agents twice as dogmatic ($\lambda_d:0.35\to0.8$) does not shift the picture at all
(Fig.~\ref{fig:res-lockin}). The transition is genuinely a boundary, not a gradual fade: the
run-to-run spread of outcomes, which is large exactly where a run can tip either way, spikes in a
narrow band around \emph{inter}$\approx10^{-3}$ and is flat on both sides. Why is contact so
corrosive? Because fusion shares \emph{evidence}, not just conclusions: an open vanguard of just
$5\%$ of the population lifts end-state conversion from $0.60$ to $0.69$, because the vanguard's
anomalous data flows across the trust channels and sets off each dogmatist's \emph{own} crisis. An
agent can silence its own instruments; it cannot silence its neighbours'. Gating cannot defeat
connection.

A control pins down what does the stalling. Keep conviction exactly as high but switch the gate
off---the agents stay just as motivated, but keep listening---and the community revolts in full
(revolted fraction $1.0$, versus $0.0$ once the gate silences the disconfirming channels,
$\gamma\to1$). So the evidence was never absent or out of reach: what stalls a community is
conviction closing the channels that would deliver it. Lock-in is derived, not assumed---it appears
exactly when enough disconfirming precision is silenced (Eq.~\ref{eq:gamma}) that the data lose
their grip on the agent's commitments.

\paragraph{Discovery waits on evidence; keeping it requires discovering together.}
Discovery in this model means waking a node---oxygen---that no agent's network yet contains, and
since one cannot search for a concept one has not conceived, candidates arrive at random (Poisson
rate $r$, \S\ref{sec:structurelearning}). Individually this behaves as a waiting process should:
the fraction of the open community that discovers within the horizon follows the textbook law
$1-(1-r)^T$, rising from $44\%$ at $r=0.01$ to $100\%$ at $r=0.16$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:res-waiting},
left). The \emph{first} discovery, however, is not rate-limited at all: with $\sim$40 agents
drawing in parallel, an attempt arrives almost immediately at every rate, and the median first
discovery sits at $t\approx82$ whether proposals are rare, frequent, or deterministic (right
panel)---its date is set by when the post-reduction residual earns a positive Bayes factor, not by
the arrival process. The community's first discovery waits on evidence, not on luck. What the rate
\emph{does} govern is survival. A discovery made by one agent at a time does not survive (the red
curve): at $r\le0.04$ the lone discoverer's oxygen node is averaged away every time---the moment it
fuses beliefs with peers who lack the concept, the shared prior treats the slot the others do not
represent as a firmly held ``no such thing,'' and pulls the awakened coupling back to zero.
Survival appears only once arrivals are frequent enough to wake the community within a few steps of
one another: $31\%$ of runs at $r=0.16$, matching the simultaneous-proposal limit ($28\%$). A new
concept does not need a critical mass of believers eventually; it needs them at the same
\emph{time}. (On the fusion scheme this result rides on, see \S\ref{sec:res-staircase}.)

\paragraph{The cycle is robust, and the discovery check carries no tuned threshold.}
Re-running the full arc across forgetting and observation noise ($\omega\in[0.9,1.0]$,
$\sigma_o\in[0.25,1.0]$) leaves crisis and reduction untouched everywhere---including the
no-forgetting wall $\omega=1$---and discovery essentially complete at every $\omega<1$. At the wall
itself discovery thins ($55\%$/$43\%$/$16\%$ of the open community at
$\sigma_o=0.25$/$0.5$/$1.0$): with nothing ever forgotten, the accumulated precision is so
entrenched that the woken node struggles to earn a positive Bayes factor against it. A community
that never forgets still sees its paradigm die; what it loses is the capacity to grow the
successor. Nor is there an acceptance bar to miscalibrate: the wake's sole
accept test is the model log Bayes factor, which rejects proposals the residual does not support
(Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery}). A separate residual-floor gate---demanding the unexplained
error clear some height before a new node is even entertained---would duplicate that test with a
free constant, and a sensitivity probe shows the constant is dangerous: set above the prediction
error the dead paradigm actually leaves behind after reduction, it suppresses every proposal and
discovery dies almost everywhere. The model instead has the property that gate would have had to be
tuned to imitate: an agent entertains new structure at exactly the level of error its old structure
produces, because that error is what the Bayes factor weighs.

\subsection{Limitations and future work}
\label{sec:res-staircase}

The honest boundary of the model is easy to draw because its two threshold events are derived:
crisis and discovery are the move ledger of Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery}, and whatever the
derivation does not fix is named there as a commitment.

\paragraph{Model expansion is the narrow waist.}
Everything revolutionary in the model passes through one move: waking a single, pre-allocated slot
(oxygen), wired to a fixed candidate set, in the direction of the leading eigenvector of a
windowed residual. That is the thinnest possible model of conceptual innovation. The candidate
space is not searched, it is given; a real discovery process entertains many structures at
once---several hubs, new couplings among existing commitments, new observation channels---and the
rank-one reading of the residual is exact only for a single hidden common cause. The acceptance
rule is equally minimal: the wake is kept iff the model log Bayes factor is positive---curiosity
(the epistemic gain $\Delta G$) pays for \emph{attempting} the inversion, evidence for
\emph{keeping} the result---which collapses into one step what is more naturally a wake-then-prune
cycle in which a community entertains structures on credit before the data settle them
(\S\ref{sec:structurelearning}). And the arrival of candidates is a constant-rate Poisson
process---the maximally agnostic stand-in for a generative process the model does not have,
deliberately so (one cannot plan toward an unconceived dimension), but a stand-in nonetheless.
Appendix~\ref{app:exploration-future} sketches the two refinements we consider most promising: a
mutually-exciting (Hawkes-type) proposal rate riding the trust graph, the mechanism by which a
community could manufacture the near-simultaneity that concept survival requires; and the rate as
an updatable hyperprior on the agent's own model adequacy, driven by the residual the discovery
check already records.

\paragraph{What remains stipulated.}
Appendix~\ref{app:crisis-discovery} enumerates what the derivation does not fix---the
operationalization of crisis, the ordering of crisis before expansion, the estimator horizons. One
commitment deserves the main text: the conviction source $u$ is exogenous and static. Letting $u$
accrete onto structurally entrenched commitments---value aligning with conservatism as a programme
matures---would recover Lakatos's progressive and degenerating problemshifts and meet Laudan's
reticulated co-variation of theory, method, and aim \citep{lakatos1970,laudan1984}.

\paragraph{Fusion decides what survives, and fusion is a modelling choice.}
The staggered-discovery death of Fig.~\ref{fig:res-waiting} rides on posterior fusion pooling the
unconceived slot's pin as if it were a firmly held ``no such thing.'' That reads naturally as
incommensurability---a shared conceptual scheme suppressing concepts it does not contain---but
fusion schemes that exclude unrepresented dimensions would soften the effect, and the survival
threshold for a new concept would move with them. Which pooling rule real communities implement is
an empirical question this model can only pose.

\paragraph{The staircase lives in observability, and what that localizes.}
The structural account predicts a belt-first, core-last \emph{staircase}: the cheap periphery
reorganizes first, the costly core only after the crisis. Where the staircase comes from turns out
to be sharply diagnostic. A conservatism-gated revision \emph{rate} does not produce it---the
high-$\kappa$ nodes reorganize first, indistinguishably from uniform and shuffled-$\kappa$ nulls
(Appendix~\ref{app:staircase})---because the linear-Gaussian Fisher deposit is set by the
observation operator rather than the data, so the conservatism geometry never enters the update.
The staircase appears when that geometry is placed in \emph{observability}: when core commitments
carry no direct observation rows and evidence reaches them only through relational channels, the
belt realigns immediately after the world changes and the core only after the agent's own crisis,
while the full-observability configuration---no staircase---serves as the control. What this
localizes as the live limitation is the operator-set deposit itself: couplings here are selected
among, not estimated from data, so structure learning is model comparison over a parameterized
family rather than discovery of arbitrary structure. Each experiment ships its own null and
positive controls (a world that never shifts produces neither crisis nor discovery; the gated,
isolated community stays locked; Poisson discovery follows the waiting-time law), and the
provenance of the sweep numbers is flagged where they are quoted (\S\ref{sec:res-kuhn}).

\paragraph{Finally, the model is stylized.}
The nets are hand-wired to expose the geometry, not fitted to data; the dials of
Table~\ref{tab:dials} are swept, not inferred; and a community that ``tracks'' the eventually-true
theory is committed to it, not thereby correct. What clears the ``more than a redescription'' bar
is not any single result but the conjunction a scalar credence cannot voice: the Schur-complement
degeneracy (a scalar has no off-diagonal to fill), the derived-not-imposed lock-in with its
deaf-but-honest control, and an expansion that wakes an unconceived node only when the world makes
its absence felt---now with no tuned threshold anywhere between the evidence and the act.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
% ======================================================================

Everything distinctive follows from taking the hidden structure seriously: a conservatism gradient
derived rather than assigned, an exploration drive that is the value of expanding the model rather
than a tuned bonus, a conviction field that is the same operator on a different source, and a
population arc---crisis, revolution, lock-in, or release---that emerges as threshold crossings of the
agents' own evidence. The sweep adds the population-scale phenomenology: the lock-in boundary lives
at very weak coupling, so the protective strategies internal to a community (conviction, gating) are
dominated by the single external variable of contact; and the survival of a new concept is governed
not by how many eventually conceive it but by how nearly in time they do.

We should be candid about which philosopher this picture serves: Lakatos, more than Kuhn. The
conservatism geometry \emph{derives} his hard core and protective belt---a node is costly to revise
because much depends on it---rather than decreeing them \citep{lakatos1970}. Identifying the
carry-over cascade with Kuhnian \emph{incommensurability} is the framing's most contestable move: a
directed Bayes net makes incommensurability commensurable by construction---one precision space,
revolution a long traverse rather than a gestalt switch---whereas Kuhn's incommensurability is
precisely the absence of the common measure the network presupposes \citep{kuhn1983}. Where the
staircase lives (\S\ref{sec:res-staircase}) is the tell: it appears only when the core is shielded
from direct observation, and graded reorganization on fixed, fully observed wiring is a Lakatosian
problemshift, not a Kuhnian reconstitution. Yet the sweep's survival result earns a piece of it
back: the fusion that crushes a staggered discovery is a shared conceptual scheme suppressing a
concept it does not contain---incommensurability as a population-level force, not a stipulated
incomparability.

Finally, the unconceived marks the limit of structure learning itself. Structure learning navigates
beautifully within the dimensions a model posits but is blind to the dimension no one has
proposed---Stanford's problem of unconceived alternatives \citep{stanford2006}: not uncertainty over
which known structure is right, but whether the space of known structures contains the truth at all.
One cannot place a posterior over the unconceived; running an expansion process that keeps proposing
nodes at some Poisson rate is what a healthy epistemic system does instead of pretending it can.
\S\ref{sec:res-kuhn} shows both edges of that blade: too low a rate leaves the dimension undiscovered
within the horizon, and a discovery made alone does not survive the communal prior. The hidden
structure of the world, which forced us into structure learning on the first page, is in the end the
thing no amount of structure learning can guarantee to have caught.

\section{The operator, the tilt, and the Schur residue}
\label{app:operator}
% ======================================================================

\paragraph{Linear-Gaussian construction.} The couplings form a structural equation model $x=Ax+\zeta$,
$\zeta\sim\mathcal N(0,D^{-1})$ with $A$ the weighted DAG adjacency \citep{koller2009}; the choice is
for tractability, and a nonlinear coupling would replace $\Top$ by a local linearization. The
propagation operator is the resolvent
\begin{equation}
\Top=(I-A)^{-1}=\sum_{k\ge 0}A^k,\qquad
\Top_{iw}=\!\!\sum_{\text{paths }i\rw w}\ \prod \pi\ =\ \pi_{i\rw w},
\label{eq:Top-full}
\end{equation}
the Neumann series finite because a DAG makes $A$ nilpotent. We realise $\Top$ on the coupling
magnitudes $|A|$ so downstream mass does not cancel when a child opposes a parent; the signed $A$
builds the precision matrix. We index $\Top$ so that $\kappa_i$ sums row $i$; under the SEM
convention the released code stores the transpose and computes the same fields as column-sums of
$\Top^{\!\top}$, so a reader reproducing $\Top\bone$ literally against the code's array must
transpose first.

\paragraph{The value tilt.} With honest posterior $p(x\mid o,G)=\mathcal N(\mu,\Sigma)$, the tilted
posterior of Eq.~\eqref{eq:tilted} is a pure mean shift,
$q_\lambda(x)=\mathcal N(\mu+\lambda\Sigma U,\,\Sigma)$, with cumulant-generating function
$Z(\lambda)=\lambda\,U^{\!\top}\!\mu+\tfrac12\lambda^2\,U^{\!\top}\!\Sigma U$. Note that gating a
channel ($\rho_k\to0$) \emph{raises} the posterior variance and hence $Z''$; lock-in lives in the
vanishing mean-update gain $\rho_k H_k\Sigma U$, not in the curvature.

\paragraph{The Schur residue.} Marginalizing a hub condenses its influence into new edges among its
neighbours: for a common-cause structure over $(x_1,x_2,b)$,
\begin{equation}
\Pi=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&1\\0&2&1\\1&1&2\end{pmatrix}
\ \xrightarrow{\ \text{marginalize } b\ }\
\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}-\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1.5&-0.5\\-0.5&1.5\end{pmatrix},
\label{eq:schur}
\end{equation}
a direct coupling induced where the joint had a zero. Conditioning on a shared \emph{effect} (the
supernova collider of Appendix~\ref{app:darkenergy}) is the dual operation, and both fill in the
off-diagonal coupling the carry-over cost is built from.

\section{The two fields on a worked example: the dark-energy net}
\label{app:darkenergy}
% ======================================================================

This appendix realises the two fields of Fig.~\ref{fig:twofields} on a domain net: general
relativity (GR) at the root; the density parameters $\Omega_m$ and the cosmological constant
$\Lambda$ as dials; the Friedmann expansion, distance modulus $\mu(z)$ and a Type-Ia supernova (SN)
reading as a descending chain; light-bending, the perihelion precession, gravitational redshift, CMB
and BBN as GR's other branches; and a modified-gravity (MOND) prediction the community wants false.
The net is hand-wired to expose the geometry, not fitted to data.

Computing $\kappa=\Top\bone$ and $U=\Top u$ on this net (Fig.~\ref{fig:res-twofield}) makes the
two-field claim concrete. Conservatism is steepest at GR ($\kappa_{\mathrm{GR}}=10.5$, the maximum)
and shallow at the cosmological constant ($\kappa_\Lambda=2.85$, a ratio of $3.7$): GR carries every
predicted observable below it, $\Lambda$ only the distance--redshift branch. This is Lakatos's
belt-protects-core falling straight out of the operator---when the 1998 anomaly arrives at the
bottom, the cheap move is to turn the dial with the smallest descendant set ($\Lambda$), not the
ancestor with the largest (GR). The conviction field $U=\Top u$, with the community valuing the
accelerating-universe supernova reading ($u_{\mathrm{SN}}=+1$), the $\Lambda$ commitment ($+0.6$)
and wanting MOND false ($-0.8$), \emph{inverts} that order: $U_\Lambda=1.19$ (the most cherished)
exceeds $U_{\mathrm{GR}}=0.45$ while $\kappa_\Lambda<\kappa_{\mathrm{GR}}$. The two fields are
de-correlated (Pearson $r(\kappa,U)=0.25$), the visible face of the algebraic independence guaranteed
whenever $u\not\propto\bone$: $\Lambda$ is \emph{cheap yet cherished}, GR \emph{central yet
comparatively value-neutral}, the GR machinery (light-bending, perihelion, clocks, CMB, BBN) central
yet value-neutral ($U\approx 0$), and MOND the conclusion the community wants false ($U<0$)---exactly
the three annotated cases of Fig.~\ref{fig:twofields}, now computed by the operator on a domain net
rather than assigned by hand.

\section{Coupling of the two fields under model expansion}
\label{app:both}
% ======================================================================

The two fields are coupled by the expansion operation, and with $\Top$ defined the coupling follows
directly. When the agent expands its model---proposing a new commitment $N$ wired by candidate
conditionals to a set of existing nodes---the joint gains an explicit factor,
\begin{equation}
p(x,x_N\mid G')\;\propto\;p(x\mid G)\!\!\prod_{(N\leftrightarrow v)}\!\!\psi_{Nv}(x_v,x_N)^{\pi_{Nv}},
\label{eq:expand}
\end{equation}
with the candidate edges entered at free prior precision ($\alpha_e\to 0$) so that reduction can
carve them back. In operator terms, adding $N$ appends a row and a column to $A$---and therefore to
$\Top$---and appends one entry to \emph{each} of the two source vectors, the unit source $\bone$ and
the utility source $u$. The two consequences are then immediate. Every node $N$ touches gains the new
coupling precision it must now also propagate,
\begin{equation}
\Delta\kappa_v \;=\; \pi_{Nv},
\label{eq:dkappa}
\end{equation}
while the conviction field gains, at $N$ and along its new edges,
\begin{equation}
\Delta U \;=\; u_N+\!\!\sum_{w\in\desc(N)}\!\!\pi_{N\rw w}\,u_w .
\label{eq:dU}
\end{equation}
A single expansion thus writes the new commitment into both the cost geometry and the value geometry
(Fig.~\ref{fig:both}). This is the precise content of motivated structure learning: an agent
contemplating a node that would, once wired in, lend value to a cherished core---or force the revision
of one---registers that prospect in \emph{both} fields, as a carry-over cost it must pay and as a value

\section{A coarse model resolves a complex world only slowly}
\label{app:coarse}
% ======================================================================

This experiment isolates the cost of a coarse Bayes net against a world richer than it. The true
world is a complex twelve-parameter Gaussian with four latent factors (correlated, individually
ambiguous directions); a coarse agent senses only $\mathrm{res}$ random aggregate combinations per
step. Coarser is slower (Fig.~\ref{fig:res-coarse}, left): the time to resolve the world state to
within a fixed tolerance grows from $58$ steps at full resolution to $221$ steps at
$\mathrm{res}=1$, and below a resolution the agent does not finish within the horizon. Adding
forgetting floors the resolution outright (Fig.~\ref{fig:res-coarse}, right): a net that both
under-senses and forgets plateaus at a residual error that rises as $\omega$ falls ($0.08\to0.28$),
and at $\mathrm{res}=3,\,\omega\le0.93$ never resolves the world at all. A complex world has no clear
answer for a bounded, forgetful, under-sensing learner---the honest limit the hidden-structure premise
of \S\ref{sec:hidden} implies.

\section{The staircase negative in detail}
\label{app:staircase}
% ======================================================================

In the base dynamics the conservatism $\kappa$ is a static read-out that never enters the update.
The natural fix is to let $\kappa$ gate the \emph{rate} of revision: a per-node deposit gate
$g_i=1/(1+\beta\kappa_i)$, PD-preserving, so a high-$\kappa$ core node integrates evidence slower
than a cheap belt node. We tested it on the phlogiston net, and it does \emph{not} recover the
staircase (Fig.~\ref{fig:res-staircase-app}). The belt$\to$core lag stays negative ($-55$ steps at
$\beta=0$, $-45$ at large $\beta$): the high-$\kappa$ nodes reorganize \emph{first}, not last, and
the $\kappa$-gradient gate is indistinguishable from a gradient-free uniform slowdown ($-45$) and a
shuffled-$\kappa$ gate ($-45$). Two reasons, which share one root cause. First, a deposit gate
scales both numerator and denominator of the mean $\mu=\Pi^{-1}h$, so it barely throttles
mean-tracking---and mean-tracking is where theory identity lives. Second, the nodes that actually
reorganize are exactly the high-$\kappa$ mass-law commitments the relational operator refutes
directly, while the low-$\kappa$ belt are agreement nodes with nothing to reorganize, so the
conservatism stratification is confounded with move-versus-don't-move. The root cause: with an
operator-set Fisher deposit $J=H^\top H/\sigma^2$---set by the observation operator, not the
data---the structure does not move, so theory identity rides the posterior \emph{means} and neither
a rate gate on those means nor the stratified couplings can express the staircase. The negative
localizes the missing ingredient to a data-dependent observation model in which the couplings
themselves are learned (a structure-matched or nonlinear operator).
