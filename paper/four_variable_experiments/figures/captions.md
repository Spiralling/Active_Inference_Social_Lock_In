**Figure 1.** Two supplied prior theories organize the same three observable commitments. The hidden explanatory variable is interpreted as phlogiston release P in (a), or oxygen participation O in (b). Observable commitments are combustion C, gas uptake G and mass change M. The phlogiston candidate links its core directly to all three; the oxygen candidate links its core to C and G, and G to M. Arrows are linear-Gaussian belief dependencies, not a historically calibrated chemical model. Signs show coefficient directions; magnitudes are 0.8 at the core and 0.9 for G to M. Dashed nodes are unobserved. The same array coordinate holds the candidate-specific hidden variable; it is not shared evidence that P and O denote one physical quantity. Single-Gaussian controls operate on the three shared observable commitments. No aggregate of background commitments remains.

**Figure 3.** A persistent parameter gap does not preserve opposing theories in this four-variable world. Two camps of eight agents attend G or M in the fixed mixed world (C,G,M)=(1,-1,1) and communicate on a complete graph. The gate downweights social influence from agents with differing observable means; it is not a sensory-reliability update. (a) Euclidean distance between camp-mean commitments on C, G and M; the two-candidate condition reads out each agent's highest-probability candidate. The logarithmic axis displays all positive values without clipping. (b,c) The camps' oxygen probabilities in that candidate condition, allowing a parameter gap to be distinguished from sustained opposing model choices. The inherited comparison uses memory 1 for the plain Gaussian, 0.95 for the gate and 0.3 for the two candidates. Anchors also differ, so this comparison does not isolate representation alone. Lines average 20 seeds; ribbons are pointwise 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals for the seed mean (2,000 resamples of complete seed curves). No smoothing or interval clipping is used.

(a) Probability assigned to oxygen, averaged over agents within each seed. (b) Individual posterior entropy, computed before averaging agents and seeds. The first plotted point follows the first update. Lines average 50 seeds; ribbons are pointwise 95% percentile-bootstrap intervals for the seed mean (2,000 resamples of complete seed curves). No smoothing or interval clipping is used.
