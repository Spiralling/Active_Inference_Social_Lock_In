\textbf{Separate effects of the two communication channels.} Four arms share an 80-update fully observed history, then the mass signal changes from $-1$ to $+1$ (dotted line). (c,d) Mean full-signal MSE and held-out mixture NLL over completed states 451--600, averaged within each observation group before averaging seeds; lower is better. Conditional exchange is uniform over 24 agents when enabled; model-weight exchange uses $\alpha_m=.02$ when enabled. $\omega=.9$, $\sigma_o=1$. The existing network seed set 0--49 is reused for paired extension, not independent confirmation of its three archived arms. Unless otherwise specified, ensemble curves and summary points summarize 50 paired seeds. Shading and error bars are pointwise 95\% percentile intervals from 2,000 whole-seed bootstrap resamples (analysis seed 20260920); they are neither ranges of agents nor simultaneous confidence bands. \clearpage

Reference means and observation groups are identical across arms; scores accumulate without decay. Unless otherwise specified, ensemble curves and summary points summarize 50 paired seeds. \textbf{A fixed-time intervention on model-weight communication.} Every primary conditional-only seed branches from its exact state 1,500: keep model weights private or enable $\alpha_m=.02$. Conditional sharing, attention, references and all future training and evaluation draws remain paired; no seed is selected by its outcome. Gas-group mean oxygen probability over (a) all 3,500 continuation updates and (b) the first 100 updates, a zoom specified before these runs using the expected rapid transfer of accumulated support. Mass-group probabilities remain at one to stored numerical precision; their full continuation and predictive outcomes appear in the intervention supplement. $\beta=.05$, $\omega=.3$, $\sigma_o=.5$. The origin is the common pre-intervention state. Conditional trajectories and direct score streams are unchanged by the intervention; probability-dependent predictions can change. Unless otherwise specified, ensemble curves and summary points summarize 50 paired seeds.
